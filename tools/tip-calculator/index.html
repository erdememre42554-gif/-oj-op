Tip & Bill Split Calculator

Split a bill, add a tip and round to a sensible total.

Runs locally in your browserDeterministic arithmetic · rounding modes

Bill values stay in this tab. No amount or party size is sent anywhere.

Loading the local engine…

How it works

  1. Enter the bill amount.
  2. Choose a tip percentage or a fixed amount.
  3. Set how many people are splitting it.
  4. Read the per-person total, with or without rounding.

Deterministic arithmetic · rounding modes

Worked example

Splitting a 86.40 bill three ways with 12%

Tip is 10.37 and the total is 96.77, which is 32.26 per person. Rounding up to the nearest unit gives 33 each, and the tool shows the resulting overpayment.

Privacy & limitations

What it does

  • Tip percentage or fixed tip amount
  • Split between 1 and 50 people
  • Round up to the nearest unit
  • Shows tip, total and per-person amounts

What it will not do

  • Rounding is a convenience, not a payment rule; the exact split is always shown alongside.
  • Service charges already included in the bill are not detected.

Want to verify the privacy claim yourself? Open DevTools → Network, clear the log, then use this tool. No request carrying your input appears. See the full explanation.

Questions people actually ask

Should I tip on the pre-tax or post-tax amount?

That is a local custom. Enter whichever base you use; the tool does not assume.

Does it handle uneven splits?

No. It divides equally. Uneven splits need a different calculation.

What does rounding do?

It rounds each person's share up to the chosen unit and shows the exact share too.
