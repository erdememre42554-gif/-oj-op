Changelog

Changelog

Changes to tools, training and the build, newest first. Cosmetic refactors are not listed; behaviour changes and corrections are.

1.0.1

Accessibility and edge-case pass

2026-10-29
  • Age calculator: 29 February birthdays are observed on 1 March in non-leap years, and the note on the page states it.
  • Date difference: inclusive counting now includes the end date in the business-day total as well as the calendar total.
  • Barcode generator: EAN-13, EAN-8 and UPC-A inputs of the wrong length are rejected with the expected digit count instead of being padded.
  • QR reader: payloads beginning with javascript: or data: are flagged and never offered as links.
  • Every tool page now links the article that covers it, and training pages list their sources before the photography credits.
  • Keyboard focus styles checked on range inputs, colour inputs and the tool action bar.

1.0.0

Launch

2026-09-10
  • Twenty browser-local tools across text, generators and calculators.
  • Split sitemaps for tools, training, categories and static pages.
  • Seven training articles with sources and Unsplash photography.
  • Local processing trust centre and plain-language privacy summary.
  • No account system, no third-party scripts, no analytics endpoint.

What is not listed

Typo fixes that do not change meaning, dependency patch bumps with no behavioural effect and internal refactors are not recorded here. A change that alters a number a user sees, the validity of an input, or a privacy claim is always listed.
