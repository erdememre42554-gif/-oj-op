Privacy policy

This policy covers the DayGrid website and its browser-local tools. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Last updated: 10 September 2026

1. Who is responsible

DayGrid (“the site”, “we”) is an independent website published at daygrid.app. For privacy questions, the contact address is hello@daygrid.app. There is no registered company address published on this page because there is no walk-in office; email is the working channel.

2. The short version

  • Tool inputs are processed in your browser and are not transmitted to us.
  • There are no accounts, no profiles and no advertising identifiers.
  • We set no cookies for advertising or analytics.
  • Optional usage counts are stored in your browser's localStorage and never sent to us.
  • Our hosting provider processes standard request logs to serve and protect the site.

3. Data processed in your browser

Text you paste, numbers you enter, passwords and passphrases you generate, QR payloads you encode or decode, camera frames used for scanning, and images you open in the QR reader are processed by JavaScript running in your tab. They are not sent to our servers. We could not read them even if we wanted to, because no endpoint accepts them.

These values remain in page memory until you clear the field, refresh, navigate away or close the tab. If you download an output file, your browser saves it to your device and it is outside our control.

4. Local storage

The site may store a small record in your browser's localStorage under a DayGrid key. It contains aggregate event names — for example “a tool was opened” and a per-tool counter — and a date bucket. It does not contain tool inputs, generated secrets, payloads, file names or file contents.

This record is used only to order the popular page honestly on your own device. It is not transmitted, not combined with other users' data, and not used for advertising. You can delete it from that page or by clearing site data in your browser settings.

5. Server logs

The site is served as static files by a hosting provider. Like any web server, it processes the request needed to deliver a page: IP address, requested path, referrer where sent, user agent, and timestamp. These logs are used for delivery, security and abuse prevention, and are retained according to the provider's own schedule. They do not contain tool inputs.

6. Third-party requests

Training articles and the homepage display photographs hosted by Unsplash's image CDN (images.unsplash.com). Loading a page that contains a photograph makes a request to that CDN, which necessarily sees your IP address and user agent. Unsplash's own privacy policy governs that request. Fonts and scripts used by the site are served from the site's own domain; there is no tag manager, advertising network or embedded video player.

Links to external sources open in a new tab and are governed by the destination site's policy. We do not control those sites.

7. Camera permission

The QR reader can request camera access through getUserMedia, and only after you press the start button. The browser shows its own permission prompt. Video frames are drawn to an in-memory canvas for decoding and are discarded; no frame is recorded, stored or uploaded. Closing the tool or pressing stop ends the camera track. You can use the file picker instead and never grant camera access.

8. Legal bases (where GDPR applies)

Tool processing happens on your device and is not a transfer to us. For the limited processing we do carry out:

  • Legitimate interests — serving the site securely and preventing abuse, which requires ordinary server logs.
  • Consent — camera access, which you grant or refuse through the browser prompt and may revoke at any time.
  • Legal obligation — responding to lawful requests where we are required to do so.

9. Retention

We do not hold a database of tool inputs, because none is transmitted. Server logs are retained by the hosting provider for the period stated in its own policy and then deleted or rotated. Email you send us is kept only as long as needed to resolve the matter and to keep a record of a correction.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to processing, to restrict it, or to complain to a supervisory authority. Because we hold almost no personal data, the practical route for most requests is to clear your browser storage and contact us about any email you have sent.

Requests can be sent to hello@daygrid.app. We may ask for enough information to locate the relevant correspondence, and we will not ask for tool inputs.

11. Children

The site is a general-purpose utility collection and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. There are no accounts, chat features or user-generated profiles.

12. International transfers

Static files are delivered through a hosting provider and a CDN, which may serve requests from locations outside your country. Any such transfer is part of ordinary content delivery; tool inputs are not part of it.

13. Changes to this policy

If the architecture changes in a way that affects this policy — for example if a network-dependent tool were added — this page will be updated before the change ships, and the change will be listed in thechangelog. The date at the top of this page records the last revision.
